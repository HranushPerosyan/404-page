❓ Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our artificial lawn solutions. Can't find your answer? Reach out to our friendly team.

How long does artificial grass last?

+
Our premium artificial grass is designed to last 15-20 years with normal use. We use UV-stabilized materials that resist fading, and our warranty covers manufacturing defects for up to 15 years. With proper installation and minimal care, your lawn will look stunning for decades.

Is artificial grass safe for children and pets?

+
Absolutely! Our artificial grass is made from non-toxic, lead-free materials that are completely safe for children and pets. It's hypoallergenic (no pollen!), soft underfoot, and easy to clean. Pets love it, and many of our customers specifically choose it for their furry family members.

How much does artificial lawn cost compared to natural grass?

+
While the initial investment for artificial grass is higher than seeding natural grass, it pays for itself quickly. Natural lawns cost $2,000+ per year in water, mowing, fertilizing, and treatments. Artificial grass eliminates all these costs. Most customers recover their investment within 2-3 years and enjoy decades of free, beautiful lawn afterward.

How long does installation take?

+
Most residential installations are completed within 1-3 days depending on the size and complexity of the area. This includes site preparation, base installation, turf laying, and finishing. We work efficiently to minimize disruption to your daily routine while ensuring a perfect result.

Does artificial grass drain properly?

+
Yes! Our installation includes a professional drainage system. The turf has perforated backing that allows water to drain through, and we prepare the base with proper grading and drainage materials. Rain, pet washing, and irrigation all drain naturally — no pooling or waterlogging.

Will artificial grass fade in the sun?

+
Our artificial grass is manufactured with advanced UV stabilization technology that prevents fading and discoloration. Even after years of direct sunlight exposure, your lawn will maintain its vibrant green color. We're so confident in this that it's covered under our warranty.

What maintenance does artificial grass require?

+
Very minimal! Simply rinse occasionally with a hose, remove debris like leaves with a blower or brush, and that's it. No mowing, no fertilizing, no watering, no pesticides. For pet owners, an occasional rinse keeps everything fresh. It's truly a set-and-forget solution.
